Page storage preservation across firmware updates is not established. Receive pages and save a project backup before updating.

Check for the latest firmware release to begin. Entering DFU mode restarts the display into its bootloader.

This device has a single firmware bank and no fallback. If the transfer is interrupted, the display may stay in the bootloader until you reconnect and repeat the update; do not disconnect, close the tab or let the device sleep during the transfer. Recovery requires a compatible bootloader and package.

Works in Chrome and Edge on desktop, and Chrome on Android. Firefox and Safari cannot run Web Bluetooth; use the .uf2 or a Chromium browser. Anything that is not Chromium cannot flash over Bluetooth. After entering DFU the device appears as a Nordic bootloader, and the browser requires you to choose the device again in its picker.
